## Why it looks like the order is ignored
1. **Tiles fill across, then pile into columns.** The gallery puts tile 1 in column 1, tile 2 in column 2, tile 3 in column 3, tile 4 in column 4, then goes back to column 1. The first row is in your order. Below that, the columns end up different heights because the images are different heights, so from the second row onward the tiles look shuffled. On a phone (one column) the order is exact.
2. **New products always go last.** "Aggiungi prodotto" gives a new artwork the last position, so OCTOMUTATION is number 24: the very last tile, at the bottom of column 4.
3. **Hidden products still count as positions.** Inactive artworks (for example "Octocubist - NOT FOR SALE YET" at position 0, and Trotella, OLIM and Get Green) are in the arrow list. When you move a tile past one of them, the arrow click works but nothing changes on the homepage.
4. **Clicking fast can undo your moves.** Every arrow click saves several products, one after another. If you click again before that finishes, the next click works from the old order and can overwrite the move you just made.
5. **The live site only changes after a deploy.** Saving changes the database straight away. octowonders.com only shows the new order after "Sync & Deploy", plus up to about 15 minutes of caching.
